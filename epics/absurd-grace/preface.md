
## **Preface**  
**Absurd Grace: A Field Guide for the Spiritually Confused, Culturally Overloaded, and Chronically Online**  

Let’s be honest—grace doesn’t make sense. It’s not efficient. It doesn’t scale. You can’t monetize it. There’s no app for it, no influencer campaign that can capture its essence. Grace is the least marketable concept in the history of human ideas, and yet somehow it remains the only thing that actually works.

This book is about *that* kind of grace—the kind that shows up uninvited in the middle of your worst moment and says, “Hey, I brought snacks.” It’s about the comedy of errors we call modern life, the deep yearning for meaning in a time of memes, and the sacred responsibility to protect what’s holy when everyone else is too busy rebranding it for a podcast.

You’ll meet prophets in sneakers, gatekeepers with broadband, and corporate theologians who’ve trademarked the words “Holy Spirit™.” You’ll laugh, you’ll groan, and hopefully, you’ll catch a glimpse of something real between the punchlines.  

*Absurd Grace* isn’t here to convert you—it’s here to provoke you, to challenge the grift, to stare deeply into the algorithm and ask, “WWJD if the Wi-Fi went out?”

And maybe, just maybe, to remind you that the truest things in life can’t be bought, sold, or streamed. They can only be received.
