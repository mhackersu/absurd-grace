
# Absurd Grace

When infinite love meets unworthy people—and doesn’t ask for a receipt.

### Sources:
- Kierkegaard, Spurgeon, Leibniz

The paradox of grace—unearned, infinite, and often seen as absurd by society.

# Chapters

### **Absurd Grace – Table of Contents**  
#### **Part I: The Joke’s on Us (But God’s Still Laughing)**  
1. **Big Mike**  
   _An icon, a microphone, a myth. A punchline with power._  
   - **Source(s):** Media Manipulation (Leibniz, Kierkegaard)  
   - Discussion of how cultural icons (like Tyson, Magic Johnson) shape perceptions of greatness and how media grift commodifies these figures.

2. **The Grift of Glory**  
   _How empires monetize miracles, and why the gospel doesn’t sell well on merch._  
   - **Source(s):** David Grann - *Killers of the Flower Moon*, Spurgeon  
   - Examines the commercialization of religion and the manipulation of faith for profit, linking it to historical exploitation narratives.

3. **Made You Look: Magic, Media & Messiahs**  
   _The spectacle economy and how we keep mistaking PR for prophets._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Spurgeon  
   - A critique of the media spectacle, where individuals are presented as messiahs and figures of worship, often for selfish or corporate gain.

4. **The Ministry of Misdirection**  
   _On megachurch stagecraft, smoke machines, and why Jesus preached outdoors._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Kimmerer  
   - Reflection on the theatricality of modern Christianity and the contrast with the simplicity of Jesus’ ministry and indigenous practices of sacredness.

#### **Part II: Love Like a Lunatic**  
5. **Absurd Grace**  
   _When infinite love meets unworthy people—and doesn’t ask for a receipt._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Spurgeon, Leibniz  
   - The paradox of grace—unearned, infinite, and often seen as absurd by society.

6. **The Math Doesn’t Math: 99 Sheep and 1 Idiot**  
   _Grace is a terrible business model. Good thing it’s not a business._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Kierkegaard  
   - A playful look at the concept of the lost sheep and the radical nature of divine grace, which goes against traditional logic and economics.

7. **The Cross is Not a Logo**  
   _Branding Calvary. Why sacred things don’t come with a style guide._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Kimmerer  
   - The commercialization of sacred symbols (like the cross) versus their original self-sacrificial meaning, tying in ideas from both Christian theology and indigenous perspectives.

8. **Holy Foolishness**  
   _On saints, jesters, and why God’s plan sounds like a prank._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Spurgeon  
   - Exploring the absurdity of God’s actions and love—how often His plans seem foolish by human standards, but are actually filled with profound wisdom.

#### **Part III: Dead Serious**  
9. **The Martyr Industrial Complex**  
   _Who gets to die for a cause, and who just dies broke._  
   - **Source(s):** David Grann - *Killers of the Flower Moon*, Kimmerer  
   - A critique of martyrdom, sacrifice, and the commodification of suffering, with a focus on historical exploitation and modern corporate narratives.

10. **Blood Bought and Discounted**  
   _Salvation is free, but check the fine print at the altar call._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Grann  
   - The tension between the freely given gift of salvation and the ways in which it is often exploited or misrepresented by media and religious institutions.

11. **Tithes, Tokens, and the Sacred Transaction**  
   _Why giving isn't giving if you're expecting a return._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Kierkegaard  
   - The commodification of faith, where the principle of tithing or giving has often been twisted into a transactional relationship, contrary to its original purpose.

12. **The Evangelical Hunger Games**  
   _Performance, popularity, and the PIP from your pastor._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Spurgeon, Grann  
   - An exploration of how the evangelical movement, often driven by media influence, turns faith into a performance for personal or corporate gain.

#### **Part IV: Resurrection is a Reversal**  
13. **Resurrecting the Nobody**  
   _How God builds kingdoms with nobodies and knuckleheads._  
   - **Source(s):** Spurgeon, Kimmerer  
   - The concept of resurrection, both physical and metaphorical, as God’s ability to take the seemingly insignificant and make them central to His plan.

14. **Backwards Kingdom**  
   _First is last, rich is poor, and death means life._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Leibniz  
   - A look at how the Kingdom of God flips societal norms on their head and how the paradoxes of grace manifest in real life.

15. **A Theology of Laughter**  
   _Why the devil hates joy, and heaven sounds like a block party._  
   - **Source(s):** Kierkegaard, Kimmerer  
   - A look at the connection between divine joy and laughter, contrasting the darkness of sin and despair with the lightness of grace.

16. **Standing on Shoulders, Dancing on Graves**  
   _Honoring the ancestors, confronting the legacy, and choosing joy anyway._  
   - **Source(s):** Grann, Kimmerer  
   - An exploration of legacy and the need to confront painful histories (like the Osage murders) while embracing hope, joy, and grace in the present.
